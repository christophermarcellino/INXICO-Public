Local data controls

Delete INXICO data

INXICO is local-first, so most chats, projects, imported files and workspace content are stored on your device rather than in INXICO cloud storage.

Delete local workspace data

Use the app's supported delete controls for individual chats, projects, files or other records when available. To remove all local app data, you can also use the operating system's app-storage removal controls or uninstall INXICO, subject to your device backup and restore settings.

Downloaded local models, local memory and local indexes are device data too. Remove installed models and individual records using the supported app controls, or clear all app storage to remove the app's local copies. Copies you exported or backed up outside INXICO must be removed separately.

Delete cloud account data

Deleting local data does not automatically delete your INXICO authentication account. To delete the account and its associated server-side records, follow Delete Account.

Connected services

Data that you deliberately sent to an external provider—for example a message, calendar event, document edit or other approved action—may remain with that provider until you remove it there. Disconnecting INXICO does not necessarily undo actions already completed on another service.

Hosted share links

If you created hosted share links, use INXICO's supported share-management controls to revoke or delete them where available. The hosted service stores encrypted share material and service metadata, while the decryption key is designed not to be stored by the hosted service.

Request deletion without deleting your account

You can remove local workspace records and revoke/delete your hosted shares through the app's supported controls without deleting your INXICO account. For help with data held by INXICO, email inxicoapp@gmail.com, identify this as an INXICO data-deletion request and describe the records to remove. Use the account's email when possible so ownership can be verified; never send a password or access token.

Removing workspace records or shares does not erase account/trial/entitlement data needed to keep your account working. Account deletion and its three-year identity-fingerprint retention are explained on Delete Account. Previously sent external AI/connector content, SDK operational data and independent store billing records are governed by the receiving provider's deletion and retention policies.

Need help?

Email inxicoapp@gmail.com. Do not send passwords, access tokens or private content unless specifically required and safe to share.

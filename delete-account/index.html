Account deletion

Delete your INXICO account

You can permanently delete your INXICO account and associated server-side account records through INXICO's supported account-deletion flow.

In the app

  1. Open INXICO and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open the account/settings area and choose the account-deletion control.
  3. Review the confirmation and complete the deletion flow.

If the account-deletion control is unavailable or you cannot access the account, contact inxicoapp@gmail.com from the email associated with the account when possible.

What account deletion covers

  • the INXICO authentication account;
  • server-side account, trial and entitlement state associated with that account, subject to security/legal retention requirements;
  • server-side deletion workflows associated with supported billing-service customer records;
  • owned hosted service records that the deletion workflow is designed to remove or revoke.

What it does not automatically delete

Because INXICO is local-first, deleting the cloud account is not the same as wiping files stored locally on your phone. To remove local chats, projects, imports and other local app data, follow Delete Data.

Information held independently by Apple, Google, RevenueCat or a connected third-party provider is governed by that provider's own account and retention policies.

Permanent action: Account deletion may not be reversible. Make any local export you need before deleting the account.

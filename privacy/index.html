Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how INXICO handles information when you use the app and related public services.

1. Local-first workspace

INXICO is designed so that chats, projects, imported files and local workspace content are stored on your device by default. INXICO does not use Supabase as general cloud storage for your private workspace content.

Information leaves your device only when needed for a feature you choose, such as authentication, purchases, connected apps, web or hosted tools, encrypted share links, or public resource updates.

2. Account and entitlement information

INXICO uses Supabase for account authentication and server-side account state. Depending on the sign-in method you choose, account information may include:

  • your account identifier and sign-in provider;
  • email address or provider identity information needed for authentication;
  • trial and entitlement state;
  • security, account-linking and deletion records needed to prevent account or trial abuse.

Apple, Google and Supabase may separately process information under their own privacy policies when you use their services.

3. Purchases and entitlement verification

Purchases are processed by the applicable app store. INXICO uses RevenueCat and its backend services to verify and synchronize purchase entitlement status. INXICO does not receive your full payment-card details from the app store.

4. Connected apps and external actions

If you connect a third-party service, INXICO processes only the information needed for the permissions and actions you authorize. Connected services can include Google Workspace and other supported providers. Provider permissions are capability-scoped, and remote mutations require the app's applicable approval flow.

Data sent to a connected provider is also subject to that provider's terms and privacy policy.

5. Share links

When you deliberately create a hosted share link, INXICO may upload an encrypted share package and limited metadata required to operate that link. The hosted service is designed not to store the share package's decryption key. Anyone who has the complete share link may be able to access the shared material according to the link's design and state.

6. Public OTA resource updates

INXICO may contact this public site to obtain signed data-only resource updates such as prompts, JSON configuration, non-executable assets and model-catalog metadata. Native application code is not delivered through this OTA channel. Normal network metadata, such as IP address and request information, may be processed by the hosting provider when these public files are requested.

7. Support communications

If you contact support, the publisher receives the information you choose to include in your message. Do not send passwords, private keys, access tokens or other secrets.

8. Retention

Local workspace data remains on your device until you delete it, clear app storage, remove it through supported app controls, or uninstall the app, subject to your device backup settings. Server-side account and service records are retained only as needed to provide the relevant service, maintain security and entitlement integrity, satisfy legal obligations, or complete deletion workflows.

9. Account and data deletion

You can review the instructions at Delete Account and Delete Data. Account deletion and local-device data deletion are separate operations because INXICO is local-first.

10. Security

INXICO uses technical controls intended to protect account and service data, including authenticated backend boundaries, scoped permissions, secure credential storage where supported, encrypted hosted share packages, and signed update metadata. No system can guarantee absolute security.

11. Your choices

  • You can use local features without connecting optional external services.
  • You can disconnect supported connected apps.
  • You can use INXICO's Offline Only controls where supported to block optional network operations.
  • You can request account or data deletion as described on this site.

12. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as INXICO changes. The revision date above identifies the current version.
